## One place for everything we build

Until today, the only way to learn what a DaemonScripts project did was to open its GitHub repository and read the README, if it had one. That worked for developers and nobody else.

The new daemonscripts.com fixes that. Every project has its own page that explains what it does for players and staff, how it works, what it needs, how to install it and which commands it adds. There are 41 of them: 36 FiveM resources from the DPS collection, the APEX and AV App platforms, our self-hosted game panel, the FiveM Optimizer and DaemonCDU.

## Find what you need

The [projects page](/projects/) lists everything with a status on each card: **Live** for what is running today, **Beta** for what still changes, **Planning** and **Archived** for the rest. Filter by platforms, FiveM scripts or tools, or search for words like "police", "train" or "NUI".

Where a script started life as someone else's work, its page credits the original author. Several DPS scripts are ports or rewrites of community resources, and the people who made the originals deserve the credit.

## The DPS look

The site uses the same design system as [delperrosands.com](https://delperrosands.com), the in-game DPS screens and our game panel: calm navy panels with a thin white edge, narrow capitals for labels, and orange only for the one thing you are meant to click.

## What comes next

This blog is where releases, changes to the DPS scripts and APEX updates will land from now on. Follow along with the [RSS feed](/rss.xml), or keep an eye on the home page.
